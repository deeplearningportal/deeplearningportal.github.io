description: Max is an undergraduate researcher at Stanford University. He studies computer science, psychology (minor) and creative writing (minor). He works in Chelsea Finn's IRIS lab on robot learning. His research and project experiences include reinforcement learning, behavior cloning, meta-learning, and robotics. He is interested in how we can create robots that approach problems like humans do. In past projects, he has created methods that use multiple streams of information, copy relevant demonstrations, and retry meaningfully after failed attempts. He is also interested in the mechanisms of learning and teaching. In his free time, he loves to write short fiction and creative non-fiction. He is working on a book that looks at the human-animal connection through the stories of animal trainers.
