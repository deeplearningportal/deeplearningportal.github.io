description: Moo Jin Kim is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Stanford University. He currently conducts research in machine learning and robotics, focusing on leveraging multimodal foundation models to learn high-performing, generalizable policies in robotics. Before beginning his Ph.D., he graduated <i>summa cum laude</i> from the Samueli School of Engineering at UCLA with a bachelor's degree in Computer Science, and later received his master's degree in Computer Science from Stanford. At Stanford, he was one of five Computer Science graduate students in the class of 2022 who received the <a href="https://news.stanford.edu/report/2021/09/24/15-stanford-graduate-students-named-siebel-scholars/" target="_blank">Siebel Scholarship</a> based on outstanding academic achievement and demonstrated leadership. His research at Stanford has led to publications and honors in premier machine learning conferences, such as the International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR) and the Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR). As a passionate teacher, Moo Jin has taught AI and machine learning to Stanford students in several courses, including as Head TA for <a href="http://cs231n.stanford.edu/2022/" target="_blank">CS231N in Spring 2022</a>. He has also served as a research mentor for Stanford's <a href="https://www.cs.stanford.edu/programs-and-resources-linxs-program" target="_blank">LINXS</a> program; his experience was so enjoyable that it inspired him to found the Deep Learning Portal program.
